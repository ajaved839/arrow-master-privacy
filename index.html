Arrow Master Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Arrow Master ("the app") is a puzzle game for Android published by ajaved839. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network features.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on your device only: current level, best stars per level, hints left, sound volume, and the player name you type on the start screen. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising

The app does not show ads and does not include any advertising software. If ads are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or other personal data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/ajaved839/arrow-master-privacy/issues.
